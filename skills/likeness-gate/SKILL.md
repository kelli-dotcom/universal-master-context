# Likeness Gate

Check copy against the owner's voice before it ships. A line that does
not sound like the owner does not ship. The gate is free. It uses a short
voice standard the owner writes by hand. Human HQ is the paid method
that builds the deep standard — the interview, the avatar sheet, the
audit. The gate is the check. The method is the depth.

## The voice standard

The owner writes it, in their own words, one short list:

- How they sound (plain, direct, warm, blunt, dry).
- Words and phrases they actually use.
- Words they would never say.
- Who they are writing for, in one line.

No scores. No numbers. Words only. The standard lives in the repo, next
to the copy it guards, so anyone can read it.

## How the gate works

Read each line of the copy against the standard, one line at a time:

- Does it sound like the owner? Read it aloud. If it does not sound
  like them talking, it fails.
- Would the owner say it this way? If the words are not theirs, it
  fails.
- Could the line belong to anybody? If yes, it comes out. A line that
  could belong to anybody belongs to nobody.
- Does it speak to the reader the owner named? If it speaks to everyone,
  it speaks to no one.

A line that fails any of these is rewritten or removed. The owner
approves the exact wording before it ships.

## What the gate does not do

- It does not write the copy. It checks it.
- It does not build the voice standard. The owner writes it. Human HQ
  is the paid method that builds a deep one.
- It does not score. Intelligence and voice are described in words,
  never scored.

## When it applies

- Every line of copy that ships in the owner's voice — a README, a
  page, a post, a product description.
- It does not apply to code, data, or copy that is not in the owner's
  voice.
